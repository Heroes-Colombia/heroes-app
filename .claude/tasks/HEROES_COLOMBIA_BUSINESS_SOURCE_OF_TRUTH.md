# Heroes Colombia - Business Source of Truth Document

## Executive Summary

Heroes Colombia is a specialized mobile platform that connects Colombian military personnel and their families with local businesses offering exclusive promotions and discounts. The app operates as a two-sided marketplace that serves both benefit-seeking users and promotion-offering businesses, creating value through verified military status and location-based discovery.

## Business Model Overview

### Core Value Proposition
- **For Military Personnel**: Access to exclusive discounts and promotions from local businesses, verified through military ID authentication
- **For Local Businesses**: Direct access to a verified, high-value customer segment with built-in marketing tools and analytics

### Revenue Model
The platform operates on a subscription-based model for businesses:
- **Business Subscription Plans**: Tiered subscription model with different plan levels
- **Transaction-Based Revenue**: Business payment processing through integrated payment systems
- **Subscription Status Management**:
  - Active subscriptions for current paying businesses
  - Free trial periods for new business onboarding
  - Renewal management for subscription extensions

### Market Positioning
Heroes Colombia positions itself as the premium platform for military benefits in Colombia, focusing on:
- **Verified User Base**: Exclusive access only to verified military personnel and their families
- **Local Business Support**: Helping Colombian businesses reach a loyal customer segment
- **Geographic Focus**: Location-based services optimized for Colombian markets

## Target Audiences

### Primary Users (Benefit Recipients)
**Military Personnel**
- **Access Requirements**: Must possess valid Colombian military identification
- **User Types**:
  - Active military personnel across all ranks and institutions
  - Beneficiaries/Family members with authorized access
- **Primary Needs**:
  - Discover local businesses offering military discounts
  - Access exclusive promotions and deals
  - Find services and products near their location or via online
  - Save money through verified military benefits

### Secondary Users (Business Providers)
**Local Business Owners**
- **Business Types**: Restaurants, retail stores, service providers, entertainment venues, online stores, and whatever registered business either online or with a physical place in the city.
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Reach verified military customer base
  - Increase customer acquisition and retention
  - Track marketing effectiveness through analytics
  - Manage promotion campaigns efficiently

## User Journey & Experience Flows

### Military Personnel Journey

#### Onboarding Process
1. **App Download & Initial Setup**: Download app and create basic account
2. **Military Verification**: Upload military ID and complete OCR verification
3. **Document Review**: Automated verification with manual review if needed
4. **Account Activation**: Receive approval and gain full platform access
5. **Profile Completion**: Add personal preferences and location settings

#### Daily Usage Flow
1. **App Launch**: Open app and view nearby businesses/promotions
2. **Location-Based Discovery**: Browse local businesses offering military discounts
3. **Business Research**: View business details, reviews, and promotion terms
4. **Promotion Selection**: Choose attractive offers and save favorites
5. **In-Store Redemption**: Visit business and redeem military discount
6. **Post-Visit Engagement**: Leave reviews and feedback

5. **Payment Setup**: Configure payment methods and billing information
6. **Platform Training**: Learn promotion creation and business management tools

#### Ongoing Business Management
1. **Promotion Planning**: Design new promotional campaigns
2. **Campaign Launch**: Activate promotions and monitor performance
3. **Customer Interaction**: Respond to reviews and engage with customers
